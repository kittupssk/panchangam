Town offsets come from Tangirala's table of time differences from Rajahmundry. As in the book, + means earlier and − means later: Visakhapatnam +6 turns 8:40 into 8:34, Hyderabad −13 turns it into 8:53. You can type your own offset and press Recalculate.

Horas follow your spreadsheet: the first day hora starts at Rajahmundry's sunrise moved by your town's offset, the first night hora at the adjusted sunset, and each hora is one twelfth of the day or night. Hora lords run Surya, Sukra, Budha, Chandra, Sani, Guru, Kuja, starting with the weekday's lord at sunrise.

Sunrise and sunset are for Rajahmundry, the place Tangirala is printed for, worked out the traditional way (the Sun's centre on the horizon). They are tuned to the book's printed times from 30 Oct 2026 to Ugadi, 7 Apr 2027, and agree within 2 minutes.

Durmuhurtham splits the day (and night) into 15 muhurtas and uses the same adjusted sunrise and sunset as the horas.

Tithi and nakshatra use Surya Siddhanta positions of the Sun and Moon. Like the book, the calculated moments are treated as local sundial time and converted to clock time. Checked against every day of Tangirala from 30 Oct 2026 to 7 Apr 2027: tithi end times are typically within 3 minutes and nakshatra and Varjyam within 3 to 4, with a few days up to 9 minutes apart. Their end times are the same moment everywhere, so the local adjustment is left off unless you tick the box. Varjyam uses the standard starting ghati for each nakshatra, all 27 confirmed against the book, including Moola's second Varjyam.

Times are Indian Standard Time. +1 means the next calendar day.
